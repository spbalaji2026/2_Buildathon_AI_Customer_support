
# SmartCart Shipping Policy

Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 business days after an order is dispatched.

Express delivery takes 1 to 2 business days after dispatch.

Customers can track their orders using the tracking link sent to their registered email address.

If an order is delayed beyond the estimated delivery date, customers should contact SmartCart support with their order ID.

SmartCart support will investigate delayed deliveries and provide a status update within 2 business days.

If an order is lost during shipping, the support team will review the case for a replacement or refund.

Shipping charges are displayed at checkout before payment.
