
# SmartCart Refund and Return Policy

Customers may request a return within 30 calendar days of receiving an eligible product.

To request a return, customers must provide their order ID and reason for return.

Customers can initiate a return from My Orders in their SmartCart account.

Eligible returned products must include their original accessories and packaging.

After the returned item passes inspection, the refund will be initiated within 5 business days.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

Customers who receive damaged or defective products may request a replacement or refund within 30 calendar days of delivery.

Customers should provide photographs of damaged products when submitting their request.

Refunds and replacements are subject to verification by the support team.
