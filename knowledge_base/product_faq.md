
# SmartCart Product FAQ

Customers can view product specifications and warranty information on the product details page.

If a product arrives damaged, customers should report the issue within 30 calendar days of delivery.

Customers requesting replacement of damaged products should provide their order ID and photographs of the damage.

Replacement requests are reviewed by the support team after verification.

If a replacement product is unavailable, the customer may request a refund under the refund policy.

Product availability is displayed on the SmartCart website.

Customers should follow the manufacturer's instructions for product installation, usage and maintenance.

For product defects covered by a manufacturer's warranty, customers may contact SmartCart support for warranty assistance.
