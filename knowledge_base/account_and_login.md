
# SmartCart Account and Login Policy

Customers who forget their password can select Forgot Password on the SmartCart login page.

They must enter their registered email address to receive a password reset link.

Password reset links remain valid for 30 minutes.

Customers should check their spam folder if the password reset email does not arrive.

If a customer cannot access their registered email address, they must contact SmartCart support for identity verification.

Customers should never share passwords or one-time verification codes with support staff.

Accounts may be temporarily locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts.

Locked accounts require support verification before access is restored.
